# Motion Lab — synthetic demonstration identity

This is a fictional, locally generated identity for a Reels Maker engine demo.
It is not a customer brand and does not represent an approved publication.

## Art direction

- Deep navy backgrounds with mint/cyan accents and white high-contrast type.
- Demonstrate restrained editorial motion, then show distinct purposeful
  mechanisms (stagger, tracking, chart progression, camera travel, media masks,
  transitions and 3D product illustration).
- Keep each shot legible, with one dominant idea and a clear reading hold.
- Synthetic raster stills and FFmpeg-generated clips only. Do not import or
  imply real customer/project photography.
- Illustrative numbers must be labeled as synthetic examples. Do not use them
  as commercial claims.

## Review and release

This identity and its demo assets are test/demo material only. Human phone
review, audio review, claims and release approval remain required before any
public use. Generated test assets are not rights-cleared customer media.
